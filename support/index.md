# FlickBox Support

For help, bug reports or privacy requests, email [support@bountyapps.com](mailto:support@bountyapps.com).

Include your app version, a short description of the problem, and the support reference from Settings when relevant. Never send an access credential or Instagram password.

## Identification is unavailable

Check your internet connection. On first launch, finish the short introduction. The Terms appear shortly after the library opens: review them and the privacy disclosure, check the agreement box and tap Agree and continue. Let setup finish before sharing from Instagram. If you previously chose Use library only, you can enable identification in Settings. A supported physical device is required for new installation setup. If you share a Reel before completing setup, open FlickBox to finish setup, then share the Reel again or tap I finished setup · try again. You can save the link locally while identification is unavailable.

## FlickBox found the wrong movie

Review each result before saving it. Try a manual title search when the caption does not contain enough clues. Recognition uses caption text, not the Reel’s video or audio.

## Delete backend recognition data

Email us with the support reference shown in Settings and say that you want your backend recognition history deleted. Removing a library entry or turning off identification does not remove backend history. See the [Privacy Policy](../privacy/) for retention and verification details.

## Keep FlickBox in Instagram’s sharing favorites

Open Instagram’s iPhone sharing options, swipe through the row of app icons to More, then tap Edit. Enable FlickBox if needed, add it to Favorites with +, drag it near the front and tap Done. Favorites are optional and controlled by iOS; the guide is also available in FlickBox Settings.
